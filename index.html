🛡 90-Second First Response

No shame and no private debate. Create distance, let the intensity pass, and choose the next safe action.
90
seconds
Move the device away and change your location.
1
Create distanceClose the triggering content, leave the private space, and place the device in another room.
2
Delay for ten minutesDo not make a decision now. Set a short delay and allow the intensity to rise and fall.
3
Ground and breatheName five things you see, four you feel, and take six slow breaths.
4
Move your bodyWalk, wash your face, drink water, or do twenty slow body movements.
5
Replace the next actionGo to an open space, message someone safe, or start five minutes of reading, study, or cleaning.
Why this can help: delay, environmental distance, grounding, slow breathing, movement, and a replacement action can reduce automatic responding. You are not required to win the whole day; protect the next choice. If low mood or compulsive behavior feels persistent or overwhelming, speak with someone you trust or a qualified professional.
